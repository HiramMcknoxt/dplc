Clean Money: Section 601.4, "Campaign Finance Limitations"

On January 23, 2026, the Democratic Party of Lonoke County adopted Section 601.4 of its bylaws — unanimously — creating what the committee calls its "Donation No-Fly List." The rule was sponsored by committee member Ethan Wilson.

"We took this approach instead of a blanket ban because contrary to popular belief, we're capitalists who recognize that not all corporations are bad; but bad actors are destroying capitalism and being empowered by ethically flexible politicians."

— Corey Spangler, Chair, Democratic Party of Lonoke County

The committee's position, in the chair's words at adoption, is that this accountability mechanism keeps bad actors from buying a seat at the table — and, as the rule's sponsor put it, that the party's values are not for sale.

How the rule works

  • Designation, not a blanket ban. The County Committee designates specific entities as "grossly misaligned in their mission, message, or operations" from the platform of the Democratic Party of Arkansas or the Democratic Party of Lonoke County.
  • "Entity" is broad. Any organization, corporation, or private individual.
  • Who is bound. The Democratic Party of Lonoke County, its auxiliaries, and candidates for or nominees of the Democratic Party of Lonoke County may not accept funds from a designated entity.
  • The list is the Secretary's. The Secretary keeps and maintains the list and the records on designated entities. The list is distributed to all candidates no later than the ballot-access meeting, and every effort is made to make it publicly available — this page is that public list.
  • How names get on or off. At any regular or special meeting, any County Committee member may propose from the floor that an entity be added or removed. The proposal is voted on at the following regular meeting and takes a simple majority to adopt.
  • Refunds. Any contribution received from a proscribed entity is refunded within 30 days of receipt, barring extenuating circumstances.
  • Enforcement. The Rules Committee investigates any suspected violation and reports to the County Committee with recommended remediation; the County Committee can approve, amend, or reject the recommendation.

The full text is §601.4 of the Bylaws.

Proscribed Entities List — maintained by the Secretary under §601.4

As of November 5, 2026, no entities have been designated to date.

The proscribed-entities list is currently empty. The §601.4 mechanism has been live since January 23, 2026, but the County Committee has not yet adopted any designation. When the committee designates an entity — by floor proposal and a simple-majority vote at the following regular meeting — the entity's name, the date of designation, and the vote will be published here by the Secretary. Until then, any list published anywhere else purporting to name DPLC-banned donors is not the official list.
