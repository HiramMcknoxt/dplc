Our 2026 platform — six pillars

The Unified Campaign Platform for Lonoke County, in short. Read the full text on the Platform page.

1. Local Control

Reclaiming local control and solving the city-limits pre-emption crisis — turning the Quorum Court into a shield for cities and rural areas after Acts 851, 548, and 314.

2. Ratepayer Protection

Digital resource stewardship: a "Pay to Play" rule so data centers and crypto mines prove they are self-funded and do not shift costs onto local families and farmers.

3. Responsible Growth

Infrastructure, drainage, and responsible growth — developers fund the watershed and traffic impact they cause, not long-term residents.

4. Law-Enforcement Accountability

Evidence integrity and law-enforcement accountability — modern, audited chain-of-custody tracking and full use of the Lonoke County Justice Center.

5. Transparent Government & Historic Preservation

Open, searchable records posted before votes, and stronger public notice and supermajority protection for historically significant public land.

6. Family Support & Workforce Development

A community literacy lifeline with local libraries, and expanded career and technical education so local talent can build a career here at home.

Clean-money rule — §601.4 status

Section 601.4 of the bylaws, "Campaign Finance Limitations," was adopted unanimously on January 23, 2026. It bars the party, its auxiliaries, and its candidates and nominees from accepting funds from entities the County Committee designates as grossly misaligned with the party platform.

Official proscribed-entities list: currently EMPTY.

As of November 5, 2026, no entity has been designated to date. When the committee designates an entity, the name, date, and vote will be published on the Clean Money Rule page, which is the Secretary's public list.
