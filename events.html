Meetings & Events

The County Committee meets monthly. Under §402 of the bylaws, the committee sets its regular meeting schedule — including time, date, and location — by vote at its first meeting of the calendar year, and the Secretary gives written notice at least five days before each meeting. All public meetings are open to all members of the Democratic Party.
