About the Democratic Party of Lonoke County

The Democratic Party of Lonoke County is a certified County Committee of the Democratic Party of Arkansas. The County Committee is composed of Lonoke County voters who file to join during the party filing period, and — outside the filing period — other eligible residents age fourteen and older who declare their allegiance to the Democratic Party. There is no limit on the number of County Committee members, and no one may be charged a fee to belong.

How the committee works

The County Convention, held biennially, is the highest authority of the DPLC. Between conventions, the Executive Committee — the Chair, Vice Chair, Secretary, Treasurer, and Lonoke County members of the DPA State Committee — governs when the County Committee is in adjournment. Standing committees (Rules, Membership, Finance) do the work between meetings. The full rules are on the Bylaws page.

A proof case for clean-money politics

On January 23, 2026, four members made quorum and this committee passed — unanimously — a binding bylaw, §601.4, barring the committee, its auxiliaries, and its own candidates and nominees from accepting funds from entities the committee designates as grossly misaligned with the party platform. It is the model this committee offers every other county party in Arkansas: binding bylaws, not resolutions — and a public list, not a promise.
