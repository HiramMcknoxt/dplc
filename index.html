A party of, by, and for the people of Lonoke County

The Democratic Party of Lonoke County (DPLC) is the county committee of the Democratic Party of Arkansas, organizing Democrats in every community in Lonoke County — Cabot, Lonoke, Carlisle, England, Ward, Austin, and the towns and rural communities between them. Membership is open to any Lonoke County resident who declares allegiance to the principles of the Democratic Party; no fee may be charged as a condition of membership.

Our 2026 Platform

The Unified Campaign Platform for Lonoke County: local control, ratepayer protection, responsible growth, law-enforcement accountability, transparent government, and support for families and the local workforce.

The Clean Money Rule

Section 601.4 of our bylaws — adopted unanimously on January 23, 2026 — bars the party and its candidates from taking money from committee-designated entities. The Secretary's public proscribed-entities list lives on this page.
